The physics

v = Cd · √(2P / ρ)  (Bernoulli)
Q = A · v
F = ρ · Q · v = 2 · Cd² · P · A
HHP = P(psi) · Q(gpm) / 1714

Water density 998 kg/m³. Ideal model: water is treated as incompressible, and standoff distance and the material being cut are ignored, so real jets differ.

Sources: NASA Glenn Research Center (2021), Bernoulli's equation; Ahmari & Kabir (n.d.), Impact of a jet; Shawver & Forrest (2023), U.S. Patent 11,808,535; WJTA-IMCA best practices via Trenchless Technology (2016).
